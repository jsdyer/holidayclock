Calendars, with context.

Source, not certainty

Canada uses Canada Holidays, a community-maintained calendar with provincial source links. Other countries use the bundled date-holidays rule library. The library’s calendar data is by commenthol and contributors, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; attributions and code licence are included. These are not government-issued legal determinations.

Some religious dates depend on local announcements. Regional, sector-specific and one-off closures may be missing. Nova Scotia Remembrance Day has separate legislation and is outside this source’s NS paid-holiday set. Follow the source and confirm your workplace's rules.

The dates mean something

“Observed” selects Canada's substitute date where the source supplies one. Switch it off to compare nominal dates. Outside Canada, public holidays are shown first; other types can be included for discovery.

The working-day planner always uses public holidays only, plus your closures. It counts each date once: weekend first, then holiday, then working day. Ranges can span up to 366 days between endpoints.

Your plans stay here

Your dates, closures and choices stay in this tab. International calendars calculate locally. Bundled Canadian years work without a provider request; another year sends only that year to canada-holidays.ca.

Exports are files you keep. Nothing is saved to browser storage. The shared CoolCode counter records aggregate tool visits, excluding form inputs.
